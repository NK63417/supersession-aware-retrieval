---
title: "INC-4890: Duplicate ledger entries after manual invoice fix (RESOLVED)"
last-updated: 2026-06-19
---
## Report
Two accounts showed duplicate ledger entries following a manual fix for a stuck
invoice. Both had the older flag-clearing update applied after release 4.2 landed.

## Root cause
Release 4.2 already retries the post internally. The manual update triggers a second
post, so the entry is written twice.

## Resolution applied
L3 reversed the duplicate entries. The stuck-invoice runbook was revised to remove
the manual update. Applying the pre-4.2 fix on a patched system is now a known
cause of ledger corruption, not a remedy.
